Why gather

Some of the most meaningful things I've helped create didn't start in a pitch meeting. They started at a table, with the right people in the room and enough time to actually talk.

Tables I've set

Rooms that became something.

Boulder

Science Galaxy

An intimate dinner I curated that connected some of the early investors who went on to fund the Impact Hub, and grew into the Junkyard Social Club, now a pillar of the Boulder community.

Denver

The Posner Center

Gatherings for IDE brought Andrew Romanoff into the work. Together we co-created the Posner Center for International Development, secured its founding naming gift and launched it with President Bill Clinton.

Washington, D.C.

The White House

A cross-sector summit for the Success 2020 Coalition, bringing capital, technology and government into one room around connecting 1.5 billion more people online.

Davos

The Digital Economist

An invitation-only evening of 250 guests at the 50th World Economic Forum, co-creating the launch of The Digital Economist. People couldn't get in.

Every time, the right people in the right room built something none of us could have planned. See the full story →
